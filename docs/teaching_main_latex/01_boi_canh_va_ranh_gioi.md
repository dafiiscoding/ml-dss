# Buoi 1 - Boi canh, ranh gioi he thong va vi sao khong thay hotline

## Muc tieu

Sau buoi nay, nguoi hoc phai noi duoc:

> Du an khong phai he thong cuu ho tu dong. Du an doc du lieu CrisisMMD da luu san, loc va sap xep tweet-anh de con nguoi xem cai quan trong truoc.

## Tu khoa cua buoi

- Tweet
- Anh dinh kem
- Mang xa hoi trong tham hoa
- CrisisMMD
- DSS
- Hotline
- Ranh gioi he thong
- Hang doi uu tien

## Mo dau bang cau chuyen

Hay ke nhu sau:

"Tuong tuong co mot tran lu. Trong vai gio dau, co rat nhieu nguoi dang bai len Twitter/X. Co nguoi dang anh duong ngap, co nguoi noi cau sap, co nguoi keu can do an, co nguoi chi chia se tin tuc, co nguoi dang anh cu. Neu trung tam dieu phoi co 10.000 bai can doc, doc lan luot se cham. Du an nay giup sap xep bai nao dang doc truoc, bai nao can nguoi kiem tra lai."

Sau do hoi:

"Vay may co tu cuu nguoi khong?"

Dap an bat buoc:

"Khong. May chi sap xep thong tin va goi y."

## Giai thich tweet

Tweet la mot bai dang ngan tren Twitter/X. Trong du an nay, mot mau du lieu thuong gom:

```text
noi dung tweet + mot anh dinh kem + nhan do con nguoi gan san
```

Vi du:

```text
"Urgent! People trapped after bridge collapsed"
+ anh duong ngap
+ nhan: informative, infrastructure/rescue...
```

Can noi ro:

- Tweet ngan nen nhieu khi thieu ngu canh.
- Tweet co URL, hashtag, viet tat.
- Anh co the khong noi cung noi dung voi text.
- Tweet co the thieu vi tri.
- Tweet co the bi dang lai hoac dung anh cu.

## Ba lop can phan biet

Ban main co phan rat quan trong: du an khong lay tweet truc tiep tren Internet. Hay day bang 3 lop:

### Lop 1 - Ngoai doi

Nguoi dung that dang tweet va anh trong cac tham hoa nam 2017.

Du an khong can thiep vao lop nay.

### Lop 2 - Bo du lieu CrisisMMD

QCRI da:

- thu thap mot phan tweet;
- tai anh dinh kem;
- thue/nhan nguoi gan nhan;
- dong goi thanh bo du lieu nghien cuu.

### Lop 3 - Du an cua nhom

Nhom chi:

- doc file annotation va anh co san;
- lam sach tweet;
- trich dac trung anh;
- huan luyen model;
- tao du bao;
- hien dashboard va hang doi.

Noi that cham:

> Du an bat dau tu ban luu CrisisMMD, khong bat dau tu Twitter/X live.

## Ranh gioi he thong

Ve bang chu:

```text
Twitter/X ngoai doi
    ↓ da duoc QCRI luu lai
CrisisMMD: text + anh + nhan
    ↓
Tien xu ly: lam sach text, chuan hoa anh
    ↓
Model: du bao informative va humanitarian
    ↓
Late Fusion: tron text va anh
    ↓
DSS: Risk, Priority, Routing, Manual Review
    ↓
Dashboard/hang doi cho con nguoi
    ↓
Hotline/co quan nghiep vu xac minh va hanh dong
```

Phan du an that su nam tu CrisisMMD den dashboard. Twitter/X live va viec dieu dong ngoai doi nam ngoai pham vi.

## Vi sao khong thay hotline

Nguoi hoc hay hoi: "Neu can cuu thi goi hotline, sao phai doc tweet?"

Tra loi:

Hotline la kenh chinh cho cuu tro truc tiep. Tweet la kenh bo sung.

### Hotline manh o dau

- Co trao doi hai chieu.
- Nguoi truc co the hoi lai vi tri, ten, tinh trang.
- Do xac thuc cao hon tweet.
- Gan voi quy trinh nghiep vu that.

### Tweet co ich o dau

- Nhanh va rong.
- Phat hien tin gian tiep nhu duong bi chan, mat dien, anh ngap.
- Nhieu tweet cung khu vuc cho biet quy mo anh huong.
- Co the co tin lan rong truoc khi vao bao cao chinh thuc.

### DSS lam gi

DSS khong canh tranh voi hotline. DSS giup doc phan mang xa hoi ma con nguoi khong doc xue.

Cau noi dung:

> Hotline de tiep nhan ca cuu tro truc tiep. DSS de sap xep bien thong tin mang xa hoi thanh hang doi dang doc.

## Diem kiem soat bat buoc

Moi truong hop co hau qua cao phai co con nguoi xac minh:

- thuong vong;
- nguoi mat tich;
- cau sap;
- can cuu ho;
- vi tri khong ro;
- text va anh mau thuan.

Tuyet doi khong noi:

> Model thay co nguoi mac ket nen dieu doi cuu ho.

Noi dung:

> Model dua ca nay len dau hang doi va yeu cau nguoi xac minh.

## Bai tap tai lop

Cho 5 cau tweet:

1. "Need food and water near shelter."
2. "Power outage in downtown."
3. "Pray for everyone affected."
4. "People trapped after bridge collapsed."
5. "Old photo from last year but still scary."

Hoi nguoi hoc:

- Cau nao nen doc truoc?
- Cau nao can xac minh?
- Cau nao thieu vi tri?
- Cau nao co nguy co anh cu?

Dap an mong doi:

- Cau 4 uu tien cao nhat.
- Cau 5 can canh bao anh cu.
- Cau 1 va 2 co ich nhung can vi tri.
- Cau 3 co the informative thap hon vi chung chung.

## Loi hieu sai can chan

1. Sai: "Du an lay tweet truc tiep tren Twitter."
   Dung: "Du an doc CrisisMMD da luu san."

2. Sai: "DSS thay hotline."
   Dung: "DSS bo sung bang cach sap xep thong tin mang xa hoi."

3. Sai: "High Priority la lenh cuu ho."
   Dung: "High Priority la dua len dau hang doi cho nguoi xem."

4. Sai: "May xac minh tin that gia."
   Dung: "May goi y, con nguoi xac minh qua nguon khac."

## Cau hoi kiem tra cuoi buoi

1. Du an bat dau tu Twitter/X live hay CrisisMMD?
2. DSS khac hotline nhu the nao?
3. Tai sao tweet co the co ich trong tham hoa?
4. Tai sao tweet nguy hiem neu tin ngay?
5. "Priority High" co phai lenh dieu dong khong?

Nguoi hoc dat neu tra loi duoc 5/5 bang ngon ngu doi thuong.
